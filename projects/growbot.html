GrowBot — LLM-Embodied Robot

Started as a workshop concept, got scrapped for time, and became my desk pet instead. An LLM-driven robot I keep building because I want to see how far it goes.

  • LLMs
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • MuJoCo
  • Robotics
  • Embedded
  • Prototyping

How it started

I'd been trying to get my hands dirty with reinforcement learning and MuJoCo — mostly working through videos and simulating things to see what breaks, which is genuinely a good time. Somewhere in that rabbit hole I found "I gave ChatGPT a body", a small robot with an LLM driving it, and it was immediately the thing I wanted to build.

We started designing a workshop around it at the IDEAS Clinic — working with the video's creator to understand the models involved and the architecture wiring them to real hardware. Time constraints killed it before it shipped. I kept building it anyway.

What it is now

A personal project, and increasingly a mini pet. The goal is to understand how an LLM can drive a physical robot end-to-end — perception in, conversation and motion out — by recreating a design that's proven to work, then steadily diverging from it into something of my own. Turning someone else's build into your own build is, it turns out, most of the journey.

Where it's heading

The idea I actually want to ship: hook it up to the WHOOP API and let it read my recovery and sleep data, so it becomes a toxic personal trainer that judges my sleep score out loud every morning. Fully embodied, entirely unsolicited feedback. I think that would be hilarious, and it's a genuinely reasonable test of the whole stack — live personal data in, personality-driven speech and motion out.

Status

Active build, on my own time — photos and design notes coming as versions come together. See also: my current IDEAS Clinic term.
