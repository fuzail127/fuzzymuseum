GrowBot — LLM-Embodied Robot

Recreating the small LLM-driven robot from the YouTube video “I gave ChatGPT a body” — working directly with its creator to understand the models and build our own custom versions.

  • LLMs
  • Robotics
  • Embedded
  • Prototyping

Goal

Understand how an LLM can drive a physical robot end-to-end — perception, conversation, and motion — by recreating a proven design, then diverge from it into custom versions of our own.

How

  • The reference design is the robot from the YouTube video "I gave ChatGPT a body".
  • We're collaborating directly with the video's creator to understand the models used and the architecture connecting them to the hardware.
  • From that base we're building multiple variants at the IDEAS Clinic.

Status

Active build — photos and design notes coming as versions come together. See also: my current IDEAS Clinic term.
