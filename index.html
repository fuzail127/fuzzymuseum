01 About

I'm a Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Waterloo who likes projects that need a soldering iron, a 3D printer, and a debugger all in the same afternoon. I've written motor-control firmware for electric vehicles, automated quantum-chemistry pipelines in Python, and taught FEA/CFD and microcontroller workshops to hundreds of engineering students.

Right now I'm building collaborative-robotics demos with a Dobot arm and OpenCV, recreating an LLM-embodied robot with its original creator, and writing rover firmware with the UW Robotics Team — alongside ongoing work where hardware meets health: an EMG-driven prosthetic claw and early research into post-operative patient monitoring.

EMG-Operated Claw

Work in progress

A step toward a cost-effective, open-source prosthetic. A MyoWare EMG sensor captures muscle activation; an ESP32 processes the analog signal and drives a servo so a 3D-printed claw opens and closes with the user's hand. Currently iterating on TPU claw geometries and more reliable electrode placement.

Programmed and characterized a two-axis positioning machine on the STM32F401RE: ADC input from a potentiometer mapped to stepper speed, polling and interrupt-driven GPIO synchronization, and limit-switch logic to prevent mechanical over-travel — precise, closed-loop platform positioning.
