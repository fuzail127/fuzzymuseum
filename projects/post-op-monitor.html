Project · Research phase

Post-Op Health Monitor

Exploring whether surgical complications can be detected from continuous biosensor data before visible symptoms appear.

  • Biosensors
  • BLE / NFC
  • MedTech
  • Research

Hypothesis

Inspired by companies like FluidAI: surgical complications have detectable biochemical or physiological signatures that precede visible symptoms — creating a window for earlier intervention than current clinical workflows allow.

Concept

  • Low-power electrochemical, optical, or MEMS pressure sensors embedded in a probe continuously capture relevant biomarkers.
  • Data offloads wirelessly via BLE or NFC to a bedside hub.
  • The hub handles alerting and surfaces flags to a nurse-facing dashboard — designed to support, not replace, clinical judgment.

Current status

Research and scoping: evaluating which complication is the best initial target based on clinical urgency, sensing tractability, and regulatory pathway. No hardware yet — next steps are literature review, clinician interviews, and a benchtop proof of concept for the sensing layer.

Why medtech? My family has an interesting medical history, so this space has always fascinated me. I put this project out in the open precisely to collect advice from people who know more than I do.
