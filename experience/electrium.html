What I built

  • Engineered precise PWM control for brushless DC motors, applying multiple motor-control algorithms including field-oriented control (FOC).
  • Programmed and configured DRV8353 gate drivers for high-speed MOSFET switching, managing high-side and low-side switching for phase control.
  • Implemented and optimized 3-phase motor control on the ESP32 using the Espressif framework in PlatformIO, tuning PWM frequencies to minimize noise and switching losses.
  • Tested under variable loads, refining duty cycles and signal timing to improve motor response and efficiency and reduce heat.
